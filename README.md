#  E-ComExpress Data Analysis Dashboard

An interactive and visually compelling dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce performance, uncover trends, and drive smarter business decisions.

---

##  Project Overview

The **E-ComExpress Data Analysis Dashboard** provides deep insights into revenue, sales, cancellations, and customer behavior across products, categories, and regions.

---

##  Key Metrics

*  **Total Revenue:** $1.25B
*  **Total Sales:** $1.77B
*  **Cancellation Rate:** 29.72%
*  **Average Order Value:** ₹112.85K
*  **Lost Revenue (Cancellations):** $525.42M

---

##  Features

*  **Category Filter** (Laptop, Mobile, Accessories, etc.)
*  **Revenue by Product Analysis**
*  **Category-wise Revenue Breakdown**
*  **State-wise Performance Insights**
*  **Year & Quarter Trend Analysis**
*  **Cancellation & Loss Tracking**

---

##  Key Insights

*  **Laptops generate the highest revenue (~$0.39bn)**
*  Mobile & Headphones follow as top-performing categories
*  **Maharashtra & Gujarat** lead in state-wise revenue
*  High **cancellation rate (~30%)** impacts revenue significantly
*  Revenue drop observed in **Q4 (Oct 2024)**
